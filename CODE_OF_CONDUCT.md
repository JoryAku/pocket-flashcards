# Code of Conduct

Pocket Flashcards welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience
levels.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.
Assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make space for others
to participate.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behaviour is harmful to the community. Concerns can be reported privately
to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
